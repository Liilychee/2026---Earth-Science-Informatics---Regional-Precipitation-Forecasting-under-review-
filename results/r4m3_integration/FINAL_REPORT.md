## 5. Skill score and strata results
- SS = 1 − RMSE_model/RMSE_clim, both on the same subset; clim = training-target month×hour mean. Single original saved predictions per model.
- XGBoost: conv 0.091 / l.-s. 0.166; JJA conv 0.103 / JJA l.-s. 0.167; non-JJA conv 0.080 / l.-s. 0.165; JJA 0.127 / non-JJA 0.099; wet JJA 0.146 / wet non-JJA 0.153.
- conv < l.-s. SS in all six models. JJA > non-JJA SS in 5/6 (Transformer 0.0504 vs 0.0517).
- Other metrics: RMSE/mean lower for conv (XGB 0.947 vs 0.971); Pearson conv<l.-s. for XGB (0.190/0.252), BiLSTM-Attn (0.208/0.229), Transformer; conv>l.-s. for BU-LSTM-Attn (0.220/0.160), BU-LSTM, Prob BiLSTM-Attn.
- No significance test of stratum differences exists (only per-stratum RMSE block-bootstrap CIs in q03e); described as descriptive.

## 6. Why "summer is harder overall" cannot be claimed
- JJA SS ≥ non-JJA SS for 5/6 models in q03e and positive in all seasons in the W=6 reruns (§4.2.3); the lower skill is specific to convective-dominated wet hours, which occur in all seasons (237 of 383 outside JJA).

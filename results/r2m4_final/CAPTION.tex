CSI of six learning models and Persistence on 3,939 test targets using the original saved single-run forecasts. Panel~(a) uses the fixed decision rule $c_\tau=\tau$; Panel~(b) uses a separate validation-CSI-maximizing cutoff for each method and event threshold, selected from a 300-quantile-probability grid and frozen for testing. Training-derived thresholds are Q90 $=0.2958$, Q95 $=0.5740$ and Q97.5 $=0.9322$~mm\,h$^{-1}$, based on 18,404 windowed training targets. The horizontal axis comprises equally spaced discrete categories, not a continuous numerical scale. Horizontal marker offsets only prevent overlap and do not change the original CSI values. The 0.5~mm\,h$^{-1}$ category in Panel~(b) was not evaluated and is not filled with zeros; fixed-rule zero CSI points are retained. Under the fixed rule, all six learning models have lower CSI than persistence at Q95 and higher event thresholds, with their predicted means rarely or never exceeding the corresponding cutoffs. In Panel~(b), all learning models except Transformer have higher CSI than Persistence at Q95. Persistence also has its own validation-selected cutoff, explaining its different CSI between panels. Paired pointwise 95\% intervals without multiplicity adjustment are reported in Supplementary Tables~\ref{supp-stab:r2m4_fixed_events} and~\ref{supp-stab:r2m4_tuned_events}. Panel~(b) intervals are conditional on the selected cutoffs and do not account for cutoff-selection uncertainty.

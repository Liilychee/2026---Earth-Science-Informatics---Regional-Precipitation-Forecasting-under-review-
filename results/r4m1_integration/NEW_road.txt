The remainder of this paper is organized as follows. \revminor{Section~\ref{sec:related_work} reviews related work on precipitation data, machine-learning forecasting, probabilistic prediction and model interpretability, and states the resulting research gaps and objectives.} Section~3 describes the study region, ERA5 dataset, feature selection, forecasting models, experimental setup, and diagnostic analyses. Section~4 presents the forecasting, robustness, probabilistic, and interpretability results. Section~5 discusses the main findings, their implications, and the limitations of the study.

\revminor{Many machine-learning forecasting models still produce a single deterministic prediction, whereas risk-sensitive applications also require information about its uncertainty \citep{yang2024interpretable}. Proper scoring rules assess the full predictive distribution \citep{gneiting2007proper}, and threshold- or quantile-weighted versions can emphasize particular parts of it, such as the upper tail \citep{gneiting2011weighted}. Satisfactory probabilistic performance over the full test set therefore does not necessarily imply reliable uncertainty for less frequent heavy-rainfall events. This gives a third evaluation gap: (iii) assessing coverage and proper scores separately for the full distribution and its upper tail. In this study, these questions are evaluated for the upper tail of ERA5-derived regional-mean hourly precipitation, rather than as an assessment of operational heavy-rainfall warning capability.}

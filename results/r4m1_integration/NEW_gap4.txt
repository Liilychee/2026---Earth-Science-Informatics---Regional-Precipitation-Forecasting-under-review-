\revminor{Deep neural networks can capture nonlinear temporal relationships but often provide limited information about how individual meteorological predictors contribute to their forecasts \citep{du2019techniques}. Attention mechanisms weight temporal representations, whereas post hoc attribution methods such as SHapley Additive exPlanations (SHAP) describe how a fitted model distributes its output among inputs \citep{lundberg2017unified}; neither measures statistical association with the target or the loss of skill when a predictor is removed. A variable may be strongly correlated with precipitation while adding little once related predictors are included. This gives a fourth gap: (iv) interpreting correlation, attribution and retraining-based ablation jointly, in the same models and precipitation regimes, rather than treating any single ranking as predictor importance.}

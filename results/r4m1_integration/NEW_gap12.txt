\revminor{These studies differ in target, spatial support, lead time and verification data, ranging from station-based classification \citep{rahman2022rainfall} and daily UK rainfall fields \citep{adewoyin2021tru} to global forecasts \citep{singh2021deep} and contemporaneous European daily precipitation \citep{otero2023intercomparison}, so their reported skills cannot be ranked against one another. Where heavy precipitation was examined separately, aggregate results did not describe tail behaviour: TRU-NET underpredicted the largest daily amounts more than IFS \citep{adewoyin2021tru}, and \citet{otero2023intercomparison} compared architectures specifically with regard to extremes. Because tree-based models often match or outperform deep networks on tabular data \citep{grinsztajn2022tree}, model comparisons also require strong non-deep baselines. Two evaluation gaps follow for regional hourly precipitation: (i) a comparison of model families under a common target, lead time, data split and set of reference forecasts, including sensitivity to random initialization; and (ii) an assessment of whether aggregate rankings hold for wet and upper-tail precipitation.}

\revminor{Regional precipitation forecasting over Ireland provides a useful setting in which to examine how such models behave. Previous work has explored uncertainty-aware recurrent modelling for rainfall prediction in this region \citep{10906190}, while rapidly changing Atlantic-influenced weather continues to challenge localized forecasting. This study addresses 6-hour-ahead prediction of hourly regional-mean precipitation over Dublin from ERA5 reanalysis data for 2004--2021. The target is the hourly-mean precipitation rate in mm\,h$^{-1}$ for the hour ending at the forecast valid time, not accumulated precipitation over the next 6~h. Section~\ref{sec:related_work} reviews how existing studies treat model comparison, rainfall-regime evaluation, probabilistic reliability and predictor relevance, and the objectives of this study are derived from the gaps identified there (Section~\ref{sec:gaps_objectives}).}

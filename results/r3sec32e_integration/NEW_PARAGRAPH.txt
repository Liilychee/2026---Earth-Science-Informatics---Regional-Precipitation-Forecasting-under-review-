The sampled values retain their native temporal meanings: u10, v10, t2m, d2m, sp and tcc are instantaneous at the valid time; tp, lsp, e and ssr are accumulations over the preceding hour; and mxtpr and mntpr are within-hour extrema of precipitation rate, not hourly-mean rates \citep{ecmwf_era5_documentation}. We aligned these fields by valid timestamp without converting them to a common averaging window. Apart from the tp target conversion described above, the selected predictors retain the native units listed in Table~\ref{tab:variables} before training-only standardization; scaling changes numerical location and scale, not temporal support.
